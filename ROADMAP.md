# Pageglade roadmap

## Now

- A reliable EPUB reading core with a quiet page, useful typography, and a quick way back to your place.
- Offline desktop and browser previews. Test reading in right-to-left and vertical books throughout.

## Next

- A library that opens quickly even with thousands of titles.
- Bookmarks, highlights, notes, exports, and portable backups.
- Read and annotate PDFs. Read more ebook and comic formats.

## Later

- Local voices, offline lookup, and optional sync through a folder you choose.
- Small book corrections with a safety copy and validation.

## Always

- Private by design.
- Free and open source.
- Friction-free workflow. One task, many paths.
- Simple first, powerful when you need it.

<h1 align="center">Pageglade</h1>
<p align="center">A calm, free, private place to read.</p>

Pageglade is an EPUB reader for desktop and web. Books stay on your device. There is no account, upload, or telemetry.

Version 0.5.0 · Development preview

## What it does

Open a local EPUB, adjust the page, and continue from your saved place. Scroll across chapters with page or section markers, read one page at a time, or show a spread. Zoom with the reading bar, keyboard, mouse wheel or pinch. Use Contents and Find to move around. Press Ctrl or Command + K and type what you want, such as “make text bigger.”

This preview is for testing EPUB reading. See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for planned formats and features.

### What "free" means here

Free software usually means two things. Here it means three.

1. Free as in "free bread": it takes nothing from your pocket.
2. Free as in "free to control": use it for any purpose, and share it with anyone.
3. Free as in "friction free": your work flows the way you like to work. One task, many paths, from different tools, by mouse or by keyboard.

The third freedom is still growing. Each new version is one more step toward it, and that's a commitment.
